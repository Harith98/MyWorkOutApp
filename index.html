          Training plan

          your week and workout templates
          Workouts

          Add exercises or runs to each workout, then pick which days it happens. Reps can be a range like 8–12. When you hit the top of the range on every set, the app suggests adding weight next time; otherwise it suggests beating last session by a rep.

          Your targets

          updates when your weight changes

          Burned at rest uses the Mifflin–St Jeor formula, multiplied by your activity: daily life plus workouts. 1 kg of body weight is about 7,700 kcal, so 0.5 kg a week is about 550 kcal a day. Protein is set per kg of body weight to suit your goal (higher when losing fat or doing recomposition, to protect muscle). Fibre is 14 g per 1,000 kcal. These are starting points; after 2–4 weeks, "Your real maintenance" below adjusts them to your actual results.
